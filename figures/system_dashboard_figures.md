## 1. Figure 4.1 – Migrant Worker Clinical Intake Interface

![Figure 4.1 – Migrant Worker Clinical Intake Interface](C:\Users\arund\.gemini\antigravity-ide\brain\3c01e4b8-b613-4e26-8a08-611325ff4859\clinical_intake_interface_1793083934297.jpg)

### Technical & Functional Breakdown
- **Unique Migrant Health Identifier (UMHID):** Automated enrollment into the national registry with cryptographic verification (`IND-MIG-2026-8841`).
- **Interstate Corridor Context:** Captures origin state (Bihar), destination host state (Kerala), language spoken (Hindi, Malayalam, English), and occupation (Construction & Masonry).
- **Vitals Intake Panel:** Real-time physiological telemetry recording blood pressure, heart rate, SpO2, body temperature, and BMI.
- **Occupational Hazard Screening:** Dynamic profiling for silica dust, ergonomic lifting strain, and heat exhaustion.
- **Biometric QR Pass:** Generates a secure, FHIR R4 interoperable digital health pass.
